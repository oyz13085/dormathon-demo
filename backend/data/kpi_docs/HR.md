# HR Department KPIs (mock document for the demo)

## Top KPIs
1. **Payroll accuracy at 100%.** Payroll and payroll variance reports run every month and cannot slip.
2. **Regretted attrition below 8% a year.** Attrition and retention models feed board people reviews.
3. **Appraisal cycle completed on time.** Appraisal and calibration data is needed in a fixed window.

## Busy seasons
- Payroll run (around the 25th of each month).
- Appraisal season (March and September).
- Annual headcount planning (October to November).

## Priority guidance
- Payroll-related, or a board people review within a week: High.
- Appraisal-cycle data during appraisal season: High.
- Engagement surveys, documentation and policy updates: Medium or Low.
