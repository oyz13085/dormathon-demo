# Marketing Department KPIs (mock document for the demo)

## Top KPIs
1. **Campaign ROI at or above 3x.** ROI and attribution analysis decides budget for the next campaign.
2. **Lead conversion rate.** Funnel and segmentation analysis.
3. **Launch readiness.** Dashboards and target lists must be ready before each campaign launch.

## Busy seasons
- Two weeks before each campaign launch.
- Quarterly planning (start of each quarter).

## Priority guidance
- Needed before a campaign launch within a week: High.
- ROI reporting after a campaign: Medium.
- Exploratory or SEO reporting: Low.
