# Finance Department KPIs (mock document for the demo)

## Top KPIs
1. **Month-end close in 5 business days.** Any report feeding the close (payroll variance, accruals, budget vs actuals) is top priority in the last 5 days of the month.
2. **Report accuracy at or above 99%.** CFO and board packs have no tolerance for rework.
3. **Budget variance within 3%.** Variance analysis requested by department heads.

## Busy seasons
- Month-end (last week of every month) and quarter-end close.
- Year-end close and audit (December to January).

## Priority guidance
- Feeds the month-end close or a CFO or board review, deadline within 2 days: Critical or High.
- Feeds the close, deadline within a week: High.
- Ad-hoc analysis with no close dependency: Medium or Low.
