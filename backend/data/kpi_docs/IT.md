# IT Department KPIs (mock document for the demo)

## Top KPIs
1. **System uptime at or above 99.9%.** Monitoring and incident analysis supports this.
2. **Access reviews completed quarterly.** Audit and compliance findings must close on time.
3. **Cloud cost within budget.** Monthly cost reports drive savings actions.

## Busy seasons
- System rollouts and cut-over weekends.
- Quarterly access review and audit windows.

## Priority guidance
- Blocks a rollout, an audit finding or a security issue: High or Critical.
- Cost and usage reporting: Medium.
- Internal tooling and nice-to-have dashboards: Low.
